# OEM and third-party data rights

- MVP customers upload material they are authorized to access for their repair workflow.
- Preserve source, title, revision, retrieval date, applicability, content hash, uploader, and access basis where appropriate.
- Original source remains private and takes precedence over AI summaries.
- Do not scrape protected OEM or estimating systems, bypass access controls, share credentials, or redistribute content beyond granted rights.
- Do not train proprietary models on copyrighted source material without a documented legal basis.
- “Integrates with” is used only for a legitimate integration; otherwise say “supports workflows involving.”
- Mitchell Phase 2 is PDF ingestion and human-verified recommendations only—no hidden UI automation or write-back.

Licensing/partnership work and customer terms must be reviewed before commercial use.
