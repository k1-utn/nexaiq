# Legal Center design

Legal documents are versioned records with status (`draft`, `approved`, `retired`), jurisdiction, effective date, content hash, and approving-user metadata. User acceptance stores identity, intent, exact version, exact attestation text, timestamp, organization, and authentication context.

The seed document types are Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, DPA, Acceptable Use Policy, Subprocessor List, Security Overview, Cookie Notice where applicable, Enterprise MSA, and Beta/Founding Partner Agreement.

No generated draft is labelled lawyer-approved. Publication requires a counsel-reviewed artifact and an authorized human action. See the root legal review checklist.
