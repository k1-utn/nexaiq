# Privacy by design — Canada-first

This is a product architecture note, not legal advice. Applicable obligations vary by province, organization, information, and processing location.

- Collect repair, vehicle, claim, workflow, evidence, and necessary professional records only.
- Keep customer name and unrelated identifiers out of AI inputs unless operationally required.
- Support optional redaction/pseudonymization for names, addresses, contact details, licence plates, faces, and unrelated documents.
- Keep employee data visible through explicit roles; do not create hidden surveillance or inferred competency scores.
- Record purpose, provider, model, data categories, region, retention/training settings, subprocessors, and DPA status before enabling an AI route.
- Support access, correction, export, deletion request, retention hold, and complaint-handling workflows.
- Do not claim Canadian-only residency until every relevant component and subprocessor has been verified.

The shop's notice/consent responsibilities and nexaIQ's legal role must be settled in contracts reviewed by qualified Canadian privacy counsel.
